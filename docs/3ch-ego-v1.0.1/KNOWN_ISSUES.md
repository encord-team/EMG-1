# Known limitations

- The raw MCAP destination count, sizes and ETags are reconciled, and four of five frame tables are not locally indexed. An unavailable frame table does not mean no video. Raw MCAPs retain recorder metadata, including account-like strings that are stand-in values, not personal contact details; L1 account fields were removed.
- Vendor segments are session-relative and vendor-derived, with no known human review. The shipped clock audits found no 10 s host-time disagreement; independent vendor-to-host validation remains pending. Use `vendor_segments(...,clock='clip')` for exact clip-relative time and inspect the `label_valid` mask for weak window labels.
- The primary split groups station-days, not people. Workers may cross partitions. The station-held-out test station has a failing left band, while its validation station has head-camera timestamp slips. Per-wrist results use the same canonical both-wrist windows, with only that wrist's features. Bootstrap over station-days.
- Quality tiers are signal rules, not semantic labels. STANDARD may need saturation and mains preprocessing. The `dropouts` package-level flag occurs on most clips; inspect durations and quality segments.
- Head image header stamps can slip. EMG t_ns_est and MCAP log_time use the same recorder host clock and agree within tens of milliseconds at starter clip ends; neither is exposure time. The recorder's 200 Hz configuration affects 184 clips.
- Wrist poses estimate optical tag caps at nominal scale, not anatomical wrists. Side association can be unverified, coverage is fragmented, and targets are sparse. `pose_valid` and `pose_validated` must be respected.
- 2 clips have PPG-only extracted files and no loadable EMG sensor inputs. They remain in the index. Their 0.084 both-wrist quality hours are included in the 271.779 h headline; 271.695 h are loadable. Video and depth can still exist in the raw layer.
- Amplitude scaling to physical units, electrode type and placement, and how the vendor produced task titles were not provided by the vendor. Ethics review, compensation details and the number of distinct workers are not reported.
- The frozen rule files `derived/quality/SEGMENT_RULE.txt`, `derived/quality/tier_rule.py` and `derived/quality/segment_rule.py` are shipped byte-identical to the rules that produced the tiers and segments; `rule_sha256_16` in `derived/quality/segments.jsonl` is the first 16 hex digits of the SHA256 of the shipped `segment_rule.py`. Their titles, docstrings and comments are the original development text: they use the prefix that begins every station pseudonym (`egox-NNN`) and refer to internal review. `tier_rule.py` also prints agreement with internal comparison files when they are present; they are not shipped, so it prints nothing extra. `derived/quality/segment_hours_1013.txt` is the 2026-10-13 hours report for the first 3,804 clips, kept for provenance; its first line relates it to the release totals.
- Some provenance attributes record build inputs that are not shipped. `source_sha256` in `derived/sensors_est/*_est.h5` is the SHA256 of the L1 file as the estimation step read it, not of the shipped `sensors/` file, and `input_sha256` in `derived/wrist_poses_est/*_est.h5` covers intermediate pose inputs; neither can be checked against release files. The `producer` attributes of these files begin with the same prefix as the station pseudonyms (`egox-NNN`), and the L1 `provenance` dataset records the source bucket name and object key of each raw recording.
- The clock and quality estimates were computed with numpy 1.26.4 and scipy 1.15.3 from PyPI, which reproduce `t_ns_est`, `clock_model_est`, `qc_t_ns_est` and the quality metrics bit for bit. Other numpy or scipy builds can differ slightly: with the Debian numpy 1.26.4 and scipy 1.11.4 packages, refits differ by at most 1 ns or one unit in the last place, and quality windows, tiers and segments are unchanged. `v` in `sensors/*.h5` is float32 cast from the recorder's decimal payload; rounding it to the payload's decimals (EMG 8, band IMU 6, PPG 3) recovers the recorded value.
- Wrist-camera IMU is absent on 525 clips, 550 wrist sides, including 25 with both sides missing. Automatic pose timing is UNKNOWN for 97.56% of image/band opportunities and 76.78% of odometry/head opportunities. The released quality intervals total 637.0 per-wrist GOLD or STANDARD hours across 136 sessions and 68 station-days.
