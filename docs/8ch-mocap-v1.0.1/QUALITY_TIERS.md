# Quality tiers and flags

`EMG_TIER` assesses EMG alone. `MULTIMODAL_TIER` adds marker, video and lag conditions. Each take gets one tier of each kind. GOLD, STANDARD and EXTENDED are labels for measured recording quality, not ground-truth accuracy or permission to ignore masks. The numeric constants are in `thresholds.json`; the complete field dictionary and observed flag counts are in `INDEX_DICTIONARY.md`.

A channel fails if any sample is nonfinite, a constant run reaches `flat_run_s` (0.1 s), a sample magnitude reaches `rail_abs` (0.999) for `rail_run_s` (0.01 s), 50 Hz power exceeds nearby power by `mains_prominence_db` (20 dB), or its correlation with another channel exceeds `duplicate_corr` (0.999). EMG GOLD requires coverage at least `gold_coverage` (0.99), no failed or duplicate channels, strictly increasing stamps, a largest gap at most `gold_max_gap_s` (0.1), eight channels and a measured rate within `rate_error_fraction` (0.02) of 2 kHz. EMG STANDARD otherwise requires coverage at least `standard_coverage` (0.90) and at most one failed channel. Remaining takes are EXTENDED.

A multimodal flag is raised for marker dropout above `marker_max_dropout` (0.10), repaired-position share above `marker_max_repaired_share` (0.05), video coverage below `video_gold_coverage` (0.99), unmeasured lag, lag-window coverage below `sync_min_window_coverage` (0.7), lag swing above `sync_max_swing_ms` (50), or observed adjacent-marker speed above 5 m/s. Multimodal GOLD requires EMG GOLD and no such flags. Otherwise, multimodal STANDARD requires EMG GOLD or STANDARD, video coverage at least `video_standard_coverage` (0.90), and maximum marker dropout at most `marker_standard_max_dropout` (0.30). Remaining takes are EXTENDED. These are take-level rules; invalid samples and marker coordinates still require their masks.

The index has 775 EMG GOLD, 137 STANDARD and 8 EXTENDED takes; 346 multimodal GOLD, 526 STANDARD and 48 EXTENDED. These counts are regenerated by `python -B tools/index_dictionary.py .`. Flags true for every take include camera sensor payloads without a validated decoder, no depth, uncertain physical band identity and anatomical mapping, native bundles absent, unmatched video rows, and video gaps. `marker_speed_artifact_proxy` is the same observation as `has_marker_speed_artifact`.
