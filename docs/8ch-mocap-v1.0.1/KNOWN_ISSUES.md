# Known issues, v1.0.1

The quality tiers and observed prevalence of every index flag are in `QUALITY_TIERS.md` and `INDEX_DICTIONARY.md`. A true flag describes a take-level observation; use the sample and marker masks for individual windows. Partial sensor gaps, noisy or flat EMG channels, marker dropout, automatic vendor repairs, unmatched video rows and uncertain lag remain in the release.

Every `take.h5` file, including the starter copies, stores `"access": "controlled access under a data-use agreement"` in its `meta/summary` JSON, and every `take.mcap` file repeats it in its `/meta` message; this value comes from the original packaging, predates the open release and is superseded by `LICENSE`: v1.0.1 is released under CC BY 4.0 (data) and Apache-2.0 (code). The take files were not rewritten for the open release, so their content and checksums are unchanged.

Marker identity corrections are automatic geometric checks, not human labels. The canonical representation may swap an entire tip and middle-end marker trajectory when evidence supports that swap, or mask ambiguous frames. In one take an unresolved pinky marker sequence is masked in the canonical array; original observations remain accessible. The 20 finger targets should always be read with `canonical_valid`. The OP-002 base and knuckle reversal is corrected in canonical markers. For a digit flagged mixed on T23, failing frames mask only the tip marker; the mid-chain markers that test compares remain marked valid. `markers/canonical_aliases` is in vendor order and must not be zipped with `canonical_labels`.

Rejected takes decided after the curated rejection taxonomy carry the vendor's short decision reason (for example "redo take" or "coverage issue") in `selection_waterfall.json` with category `vendor_decision_reason_uncurated`; these reasons were not reviewed or regrouped. Rejections with no vendor reason have no category.

Band-to-mocap lag is unmeasured or boundary-unmeasured for some takes. The loader applies no shift in those cases. Even measured lag is a band IMU timing estimate rather than EMG latency. The reported window score, coverage and swing are quality indicators, not confidence intervals. Camera sensor payloads have no validated decoder. Physical band identity is unconfirmed. The band is worn high on the forearm below the elbow (owner report); the electrode type, exact placement and channel order were not provided by the vendor. EMG values are normalized ADC counts; physical amplitude units (volts) and hardware filters were not provided by the vendor. No depth was captured. Native TAK and SVO2 files are not included in v1.0.1.

A lag window counts as good when its fit score exceeds 0.5, including windows whose best lag sits at the 300 ms search limit; ignoring such windows would shift the applied lag by up to about 11 ms in about 2% of takes. Subject-relative clock placement uses session anchors from the vendor logs, so a vendor re-decision of one recording can move the epoch of sibling takes (see `CHANGELOG.md`); `epoch_start_uncertainty_s` does not cover such anchor changes. Within-take synchronisation is unaffected.

`has_<modality>_gaps` counts gaps inside the recording window; a stream that starts late or ends early is not flagged (for example the last 3.6 s of band data in OP-001/s017/05). Band IMU samples equal to -4 (accelerometer) or -500 (gyroscope) are vendor sentinels and are marked invalid in `accel_valid` and `gyro_valid`. The C3D `POINT:FRAMES` parameter is 16-bit; for the 319 s take OP-001/s019/13 read the frame count from the C3D header or the HDF5 file.

The video is not visually anonymised and faces were not blurred. Report errata to support@encord.com; fixes ship as new versions and are listed in `CHANGELOG.md`.

The T3 scorer uses the first 10 seconds of each take as CAL. A 5.5 to 15.5 second window was examined only as a sensitivity and is not the protocol. The internal vision clock adjustment of -16 ms is not applied; it changes the current estimate-set median from 25.17 to 24.90 mm. About two of five distal offsets per take meet the 20 mm cap. Vision estimates missing early hand entry often fail the CAL gate. The automatic vision estimates are not bit-reproducible: re-running the pinned MediaPipe and stereo chain on identical video reproduced earlier estimates to within 1 micrometre for most takes but changed detections materially in a few (listed in `CHANGELOG.md`). Estimates made before per-take input binding was recorded say so in `provenance_est`; their binary video inputs were verified unchanged. Run `python -B -m eval.vision` to regenerate coverage and scores from the files present.
